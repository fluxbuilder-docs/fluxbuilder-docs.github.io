🚀 FluxBuilder 3.0 brings AI Layout blocks, the new AI Screen, a Product Detail designer, 25 new templates, new Edit and Play buttons, and new settings for Blog, Sign In and Tab Bar.

(1) AI Layout Blocks 🎨​

Supported: All Apps

  • 88 ready-made AI blocks that show your real products, categories and colors.
  • Click a headline, image or button inside a block to edit only that element.
  • Live data: pick the category, set the item limit and the countdown end time.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Layouts > AI Layout, then drag a block into the app preview:

(2) AI Screen 📱​

Supported: All Apps

A new screen type drawn from a template.

  • Start from a template: Starter, Sneakers, Marketplace or Furniture.
  • Click any element on the phone to change its text, colors and style.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Design, add a screen with +, then set Screen type to AI Screen:

(3) Screen Outline & Pages​

Supported: All Apps

  • Screen outline: every block of the screen in one list. Click a row to open it.
  • Pages: open Product detail and Blog detail in one click.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Design and switch the preview to Edit:

(4) New Edit & Play Buttons​

Supported: All Apps

  • Edit: click the phone to select a section and change its settings.
  • Play: tap the phone like in the real app.

You find both buttons in the toolbar under the phone:

(5) Product Detail Designer 🛍️​

Supported: All E-commerce Apps

  • AI Design layout: 5 page designs (Floating, Modern, Minimal, Editorial, Market), or customize one with AI.
  • Page Sections: reorder, hide and style the parts of the page.
  • Buy Buttons: size, corners, font, colors, order and margin.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Design > Pages > Product detail:

(6) 25 New Templates 🧩​

Supported: All Apps

New templates for Shopping, Finance, Food & Drink, Education, Music, Social, Sports, Fitness, Business and the new House & Home category.

(7) Blog Settings​

Supported: WordPress-based Apps

  • Blog detail: Show Tags, Post Types to Open in Webview.
  • Blog layouts: Post Type (posts, pages or custom types), Advanced Params, Hide When Empty.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Design > Pages > Blog detail:

(8) Sign In & Languages​

Supported: All Apps

  • Sign In: Screen Style (Classic, Card, Minimal), Show Logo, SMS login Countries.
  • Languages: Language Names (Native Name, English, App Language).

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Features > Sign In/Sign Up Screen or Languages:

(9) Tab Bar Indicator​

Supported: All Apps

  • Rectangular indicator: Height and Distance From Center.
  • Liquid Glass: Pill Margin.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Tab Bar > Indicator Style > Rectangular:

(10) More Design Options​

Supported: All Apps

  • Category layout: Center Last Row (with Wrap Mode on).
  • Profile screen: Delete Account item.

(11) PWA: Web Payment Method IDs​

Supported: PWA

Choose which payment methods show on your web checkout.

Enable this feature: open your app on FluxBuilder, select Features > PWA Settings:

(12) Improvements​

  • AI Assistant: now the AI tab next to Settings, shared by Design and Features. Manager and Delivery apps can use it too. Keep or undo every change.
  • Create apps: one box on the Dashboard for your website or idea. Choose the plan when you create the app.
  • AI wizard: the first preview shows your real products. Switch Sample | Your site, or Try another look.
  • Layout Kit: about 60 new Animated Stack layouts, and new block icons.
  • Preview: a cleaner toolbar, Zoom out and canvas backgrounds. Liquid Glass looks like real iOS.
  • Design screen: settings grouped in sections, a resizable panel, focus mode with Cmd/Ctrl + \.
  • Plans & billing and Credits: redesigned pages for your plans, apps and AI credits.
  • Faster: less memory on desktop, faster first load on web, works on phones.
  • Agency: fully white-labeled builder.
  • MCP: use Remote MCP. The local MCP tool is removed.
